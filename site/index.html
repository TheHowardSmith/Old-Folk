An endless radio of the world's old folk recordings

Old-time fiddles, country blues, field recordings, polkas, calypso, Karelian laments and Bhojpuri songs: public-domain recordings from about 1900 to 1970, streamed straight from the archives that look after them.

Download for Android Source code

Android 8 or newer. Open this page on your phone, tap Download, then open the file. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.

What it does

Press play and it keeps going: a random stream that never repeats a track until you've heard everything in your selection.

Endless radio

Random tracks from tens of thousands of recordings. The tuning dial's needle points at the year each one was made.

Pick your genres

Old-time, blues, gospel, Celtic, European dance, polka, klezmer, calypso, Latin American, African, Indian, Asian, Hawaiian and field recordings.

Easy listening

Every track is levelled to the same loudness, sudden pops are smoothed, one-sided transfers play on both speakers, and very noisy discs are skipped.

History and playlists

Scroll back through everything you've heard, go back a track, or save favourites to playlists.

Every track credited

See who recorded it, when and where, with a link to the recording's page at the archive that holds it.

Free and private

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Recordings stream from the archives and can't be copied off the phone.

A look inside

Styled after a 1950s valve radio, in light and dark.

Where the music comes from

There's no streaming server: the app plays each recording straight from the archive that holds it, and credits that archive on every track.

  • Internet ArchiveThe Great 78 Project's digitised 78 rpm discs, and folk LPs and field recordings
  • Library of CongressAmerican Folklife Center field recordings from the 1930s and 40s
  • National Library of Finland and the Finnish Literature SocietyFinnish 78s and Karelian and Ingrian field recordings, through Finna
  • Instituto Moreira SallesBrazilian regional 78s from the Discografia Brasileira
  • Digital South Asia LibraryLinguistic Survey of India gramophone recordings, 1913–1929

For archives and libraries

Do you look after a collection of traditional or historical recordings? We'd love to help more people hear them. Old Folk Air streams straight from your own servers, credits your institution on every track with a link back to your catalogue, and never copies or re-hosts your files.

If you'd like your recordings included, or want to set conditions, please get in touch: thehowardsmith@gmail.com

Rights and removals

The recordings are hosted by the archives above and aren't covered by this project's licences. To the best of our knowledge they are no longer protected by copyright: every track meets our selection criteria (recorded or released in 1970 or earlier, not claimed by a rights holder in the US Copyright Office's records, and no restrictions noted by the archive). Restricted, sacred or culturally sensitive recordings are left out.

If you hold the rights to a recording and want it removed, email thehowardsmith@gmail.com or use “Report” in the app.

Privacy

Old Folk Air has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It downloads the track list from GitHub and the recordings from the archives, and keeps your listening history and playlists on your phone only. If a track fails to play, the app can open a pre-filled problem report, but only you can choose to send it.
