# Archive research

Research notes on online archives of historical folk, traditional and field
recordings, gathered to find sources Old Folk Air could stream (or ask to stream).
Each report rates archives as **good fit** (stream directly), **ask permission**
(worth an outreach email; drafts in [../outreach-emails.md](../outreach-emails.md))
or **not usable**, with sources. Claims are marked verified, search-only or blocked.

**Coverage (November 2026):** the six first-pass sweeps shared a limit of 200 web
searches, which ran out partway through; four second-pass sessions then covered
the gaps (sweep2-*.md). Each report still lists hosts it couldn't reach. Many national sites
were also unreachable from the research environment (blocked, bot checks, logins);
none were bypassed.

## Summary

**Already streamed:** Internet Archive, Library of Congress, Finna (Finland), Discografia Brasileira (Brazil).

**Good fits found (small):**
- DSAL / Linguistic Survey of India gramophone recordings (Univ. of Chicago): 242 recordings, 1913–29, ~45 songs in Indian tribal and regional languages; direct MP3s, JSON API.
- Internet Archive collections: `MudritSangeet` (Indian 78s, filter to pre-1966 folk/regional), `chinesehistsocietysc` (~9 Cantonese opera 78s from 1902, public domain), `papuaculturalinheritance` (8 West Papua recordings 1955–62, CC BY-NC-ND).
- Gongu Madang (Korea Copyright Commission): a few 1951 Arirang 78s, CC BY.
- Bolivian 78s (Ajayus de Antaño, Internet Archive): 109 sides 1916–1950s, mostly CC BY-NC-ND. Added to the builder (`78rpm-bolivia` source).
- Small: K'iche' Maya narratives at UNM (spoken word, so not used), and ~5 pre-1971 items in the Soule Archive (US Virgin Islands, via dLOC).

**Strongest ask-permission candidates:**
- Africa: AfricaMuseum Tervuren (3,000 h of Central African field recordings; grants CC BY where it holds the rights; per-country counts in sweep2-africa.md), the Berlin Phonogramm-Archiv (about 2,200 pre-WWI African cylinders, catalogue only), SierraLeoneHeritage.org (236 recordings 1964–77 + 110 BL cylinders), Music Time in Africa (Univ. of Michigan). Malawi's MBC and Eritrea's RDC are digitising, not yet online.
- South Asia: Cambridge "The Nagas" (1919 cylinders; communities' wishes first), IGNCA/NCAA Rupayan Sansthan (Rajasthan 1958–76), Archive of Indian Music.
- East Asia: National Museum of Taiwan History (Taiwanese 78s from the 1890s), Academia Sinica, National Gugak Center (Korea), Japan's National Diet Library, Kyoto University's 1940–43 Kinki folk-song field discs (KURRA), ARKO's 274 Korean 78s (DA-Arts), Tibetan recordings at the University of Washington and TIPA.
- Southeast Asia & Pacific: Hawaiʻi State Archives (Rockwell 78s 1890–1960), UP José Maceda collection (Philippines), Bishop Museum, Singapore's Sounds of Yesteryear.
- Middle East & Central Asia: **Phonothèque Nationale de Tunisie (CMAM)**, which now has a public catalogue with 1,469 full-access items as direct MP3s (pre-1934 Tunisian 78s, malouf, Bedouin field recordings; terms say copying needs explicit permission), Jewish Music Research Centre (Gerson-Kiwi field recordings), Komitas Museum (1912–14), CompMusic/SNRT Arab-Andalusian, Armenian Museum of America (3,000 78s), Golistan (Persian 78s), Excavated Shellac, SALT Research (Ottoman 78s), Harvard's Arabic 78s on Aviary.
- Latin America & Caribbean: Instituto de Cultura Puertorriqueña (1909 cylinders), Señal Memoria (Colombia), Instituto Carlos Vega (Argentina), Centro Lauro Ayestarán (Uruguay), Mexico's Fonoteca Nacional and Fonoteca INAH, Biblioteca Nacional Aruba's Ito Tromp tapes (1967–70, already on the Internet Archive without a licence), the Brenes/Zárate Panama field recordings, Curaçao's Zikinzá collection, MuseCom's Casa A Electrica 78s (Brazil), Koch-Grünberg's 1911–13 Amazon cylinders (Berlin).
- Europe: CREM-CNRS (largest Africa/Asia corpus), Max Hunter Collection (Ozarks).

**Excluded on principle:** community-controlled or sacred collections (AIATSIS, closed Vanuatu VKS, Jaap Kunst cylinders under adat law, Vodou archive, secret-society chants) unless the communities agree.
