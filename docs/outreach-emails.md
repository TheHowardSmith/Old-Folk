## Base email (adapt per archive)

**Subject:** Streaming your public-domain recordings in Old Folk, a free folk-music radio app

Dear [name / team],

I run Old Folk, a free, non-commercial Android app that plays an endless random stream of traditional and field recordings from around the world. There are no ads, accounts or fees, and the code is open source: https://github.com/TheHowardSmith/Old-Folk

We'd love to include recordings from [collection], limited to items that are in the public domain or that you mark as freely usable.

How the app treats your recordings:

- It streams each recording directly from your servers. We never copy or re-host your files.
- It credits [institution] on every track, with a link back to the recording's page in your catalogue.
- Nothing can be downloaded or saved. Playback runs through an encrypted cache that only the app can read, so listeners can't extract or redistribute the files.
- It's gentle on your servers. Only the next two tracks from your collection are fetched ahead, each file is downloaded once and then played from the cache, and our list builder makes one request at a time.
- Anything restricted, sacred or culturally sensitive is excluded, and we remove any item on request.

[Specific ask, e.g. "Would you allow this?", "Is there a list of your public-domain items we could use?", or "Could we have stream access to the public-domain items?"]

We're happy to follow any conditions you set: attribution wording, which collections, or a limit on requests.

With thanks,
[Your name]
Old Folk · thehowardsmith@gmail.com

Prezada equipe do IMS,

Sou o responsável pelo Old Folk, um aplicativo gratuito e sem fins comerciais que toca gravações tradicionais e de campo do mundo todo. Gostaríamos de incluir as gravações da Discografia Brasileira que já estão em domínio público, em gêneros regionais como toada, embolada, coco e moda de viola.

O aplicativo toca cada faixa diretamente dos servidores do IMS, credita o Instituto Moreira Salles em todas as faixas com link para a página da gravação, e não permite baixar nem redistribuir os arquivos.

Vocês poderiam nos enviar uma lista (CSV ou similar) das gravações em domínio público, com os links de áudio? Seria mais rápido para nós e mais leve para o site.

Muito obrigado,
